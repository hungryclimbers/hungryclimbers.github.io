Desna Uttamchandani

Coach & Competitive Climber

Desna has been climbing competitively for nearly a decade and has had the privilege of learning from a slew of exceptional coaches. She has first hand experience adapting alongside the rapidly increasing quality of competition, scope of movement, and physical demands.

Her enthusiasm, breadth of knowledge, and detailed eye reflects her passion for all things climbing. She will push you hard because her philosophy is simple:

"The more climbs you can do, the more climbing you get to enjoy!"

Desna’s strongest suits are her incredible try-hard, indestructible fingers, and creative ingenuity.
Desna is currently based near Jersey City during the school year, and spends holidays in the Westchester area.
